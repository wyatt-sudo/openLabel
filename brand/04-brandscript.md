# OpenLabel — BrandScript (StoryBrand)

*The customer is the hero; OpenLabel is the guide. This drives all homepage and marketing copy. Built from `01-jtbd-analysis` + `03-ideal-client-profile`. Hero = the Burned-but-Hopeful Health Researcher. Tone: warm, plain, quietly confident; never mocking, never salesy. 8th-grade reading level.*

---

### Offering Name
**OpenLabel** — an honest read on what health products are really selling you.

### Tagline
**Discernment for the things being sold to your health.** *(chosen 2026-06-14 — leads the homepage)*
Sub-hed: *What may be real, what's overclaimed, and how to try it without getting captured.*
*(Alts considered: "Read the claim before it reads you." · "Hope, without getting played.")*

### Controlling Idea
You shouldn't have to choose between false hope and bitter cynicism to make a health decision. With an honest read, you can hold both hope and skepticism — and trust yourself again.

---

### A Character — What do they want?
Health-engaged people who've been burned before and just want to know, fast, whether a wellness product, device, or claim is worth their money and trust — without becoming a part-time researcher to find out.

### With a Problem

**External Problem:**
They're staring at an expensive, hard-to-evaluate health claim — and reviews are gamed, "studies show" anything, and everyone with an answer is also selling something.

**Internal Problem:**
They feel like a sucker for wanting to believe, and exhausted from being their own expert. *"A part-time unpaid job trying to figure all this out."*

**Philosophical Problem:**
No one should have to gamble their money, body, and hope just to find out what's true — and wanting to feel better shouldn't make you a mark.

**The Villain:**
The persuasion machine of the wellness market — overclaiming marketing, gamed reviews, and influencers whose trust is for sale — engineered to route real human needs through expensive things that don't earn the promise. *(A system, not a person.)*

---

### With a Guide

**Empathetic Statement:**
We know the feeling of wanting something to work so badly you talk yourself past the red flags — and the worse feeling weeks later when it didn't. We know the exhaustion of ten open tabs and ten contradictory answers. You're not gullible. You're navigating a market built to overwhelm you.

**Authority:**
- A rigorous, primary-source-weighted method — not vibes: claim extraction, substantiation scoring, conviviality and agency analysis, anchor calibration.
- Worked public audits of real products (BrainTap, Healy, Primal Trust, Sana) — you can see the reasoning, not just the verdict.
- **We don't sell the products we read, and we take no affiliate cut.** The consumer read is free. *(No skin in the sale.)*
- Built on one promise: **we don't replace your judgment — we help you trust yourself better.**

---

### With a Plan — "The Open Read"
1. **Paste the thing.** Drop in a product, link, or claim. It's free, and you don't have to sign up to see your first read.
2. **Get the honest read.** In seconds you see what may be real, what's overclaimed, what need it's serving, cleaner alternatives, and — with the evidence shown — *why* we think so.
3. **You decide, clearer than before.** You walk away knowing exactly what this offer is asking you to trust, buy, and ignore — hope intact, judgment sharper, no longer guessing alone.

### Call to Action

**Direct CTA:** **Read a claim free →** *(paste box, front and center)*

**Email Magnet:** **"5 Red Flags in Any Health Claim"** — a one-page guide to spotting overclaiming before you buy, so you can read the next pitch yourself.

---

### Failure
Without it, the cycle continues: more money lost on things that don't work, more hope spent and betrayed, the slow slide into either cynicism (refusing the thing that might've helped) or capture (handing your judgment to whoever sells the best story) — and the quiet, lonely feeling that you can't trust yourself with your own health.

### Success
You see a bold health claim and feel calm instead of cornered. One paste, a few seconds, and you know what's real, what's hype, and what it's really asking of you. You stop overpaying for stories and stop missing what could genuinely help. You make the call yourself — for you, and for the people you look after — and for the first time in a while, you trust the person making it.

---

### Transformation in Identity
**Current Identity:** *"I want to believe but I keep getting burned — so I either overpay on hope or miss out on cynicism, and I don't trust myself either way."*

**Aspirational Identity:** *"I'm a discerning health navigator. I can hold hope and skepticism at once, see what an offer is really asking of me, and decide for myself — without being captured."*

---

## How to use this script
- Every homepage section, ad, and email should map to a slot above: hero headline = Tagline/Controlling Idea; sub-hed = Character+Want; the story strip = External→Internal→Philosophical problem; an "is it biased?" section = Guide (Empathy+Authority); the paste tool framed by the 3-step Plan; a Failure/Success contrast band; close on the identity shift + CTA.
- Keep the villain a *system*, never the hopeful customer.
- Lead with the emotional product (relief, self-trust); let the methodology be the proof underneath, not the headline.
- Reference this script in all future marketing prompts to keep the message consistent.
