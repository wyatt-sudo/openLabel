## Who the "client" is
The client is **OpenLabel itself**. Placeholders in Joseph's intake prompts were filled from OpenLabel's
own foundation docs (esp. `22-positioning-and-pitch`, `14-icp-trust-and-learning-panels`, `17-mvp-synthesis`).

## Intake recap (the inputs these analyses run on)
- **Offering:** A discernment instrument for persuasive health/wellness markets. Turns a product/program/device/claim into a plain read: what may be real, what's overclaimed, what need is being served, what's being sold, what cleaner alternatives exist, and how to try it without surrendering agency. (Prototype: `ship/`. Future distribution: browser extension.)
- **Audience (consumer / hero side):** people deciding whether a health claim is worth their money and trust — across four reader positions: Curious Consumer, Frontier Health Optimizer, Helpful Healer (caregiver), Chronic Illness & Complex Symptoms.
- **Audience (B2B / monetization side):** companies wanting an "OpenLabel Approved" certification; investors/journalists/operators wanting a trusted read.
- **Delivery:** digital — static web tool now → browser extension → API/certification layer.
- **Pricing:** undetermined (the recording: "I'm not sure how you're going to make money." "Me neither."). Working model: consumer side free/freemium; revenue from B2B certification/licensing + a separate open-label-placebo product line. See `02`.
- **Unique approach:** holds the middle between cynicism and credulity; describes-don't-condemns; reader-in-relation-to-product panels; evidence-traced; agency-preserving.
- **Stance:** evidence-aware, intuition-respecting, hope-preserving, capture-resistant.

## Sourcing note (honesty)
The "Marketing Gold" quotes are **real and verbatim** from public sources. `reddit.com` was blocked to the
research crawler, so quotes were pulled from fetchable patient/consumer forums (Phoenix Rising, Health Rising,
Mumsnet, AgingCare, LongeCity, WHOOP, Hacker News, ProductReview, Trustpilot) and from Reddit **archives**
(PullPush API, Wayback Machine). Each quote carries its platform + source. A few search-surfaced snippets could
not be re-verified on-page (noted inline). Before quoting anything load-bearing in a deck, eyeball it against the
live URL.
